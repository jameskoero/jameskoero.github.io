Nyando Flood AI Flood-susceptibility ranking for five wards of the Nyando Basin, Kenya | v2.0.0, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23919053

  • Sentinel-1 flood labels (4,420 sampled points, 35 scene dates) with terrain, rainfall, soil and land-cover features.
  • Monotonic-constrained gradient boosting, exported to ONNX and served through FastAPI, with a React dashboard.
  • Leave-one-event-out mean per-event AUC 0.943 on 23 events; 0.971 on 27 sealed events scored once (elevation-only baseline 0.939).
  • No advantage shown under a 1 km buffered split or out of time. Scores are rankings, not probabilities.
  • Retracted my earlier inflated figures publicly and rebuilt the project with a leakage audit, CI, CodeQL, MLflow tracking and hash-locked Docker installs (31 packages).
