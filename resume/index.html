Selected projects

Nyando Flood AI Flood-susceptibility ranking, five wards of the Nyando Basin, Kenya | v2.0.0, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23919053

  • Copernicus GFM flood labels (35 Sentinel-1 scene dates, 4,420 real rows, no synthetic data) with Google Earth Engine terrain, rainfall, soil and land-cover features.
  • Gradient boosting with monotonic constraints, chosen against logistic regression, XGBoost and a PyTorch physics-constrained network that violated the constraints and was not shipped.
  • Custom ONNX exporter (parity 3.3e-16) served by FastAPI, which verifies the model's SHA-256 before loading, with rate limiting, security headers and a hash-locked Docker image (31 packages).
  • Mean per-event AUC 0.971 on 27 sealed events scored once under rules fixed in advance (mappable frame; elevation-only 0.939, registered logistic champion 0.950). Gain over elevation +0.038, interval 0.019 to 0.061, pooled over 50 events.
  • No advantage shown under a 1 km buffered split or out of time. Scores are rankings, not probabilities.
  • Retracted my own circular headline metric publicly (Zenodo erratum) and rebuilt the project with leakage audits, CI gates on data and claims, CodeQL and MLflow tracking.

AfriSalaries Salary-band classifier from job descriptions, 8 African markets | API on Render, frontend on Vercel
