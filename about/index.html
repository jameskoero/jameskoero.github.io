Profile

I am a machine learning engineer in Kisumu, Kenya, working independently since 2024. I build models end to end: data, validation, serving and documentation. My projects are public, and each one states its measured results and its limits.

Background

I studied physics and mathematics at Moi University (B.Sc., 2012) and did geophysics fieldwork at KenGen's Olkaria geothermal project in 2011. I taught physics and mathematics in secondary schools in Nyanza, then worked for more than seven years in Kenyan financial services sales and sales management (2013 to 2020). I retrained in machine learning and Python from 2020, and I have worked as an independent ML engineer and researcher since 2024.

That background shapes how I work: I test a model against a plain baseline, with the rules fixed before I look at results.

How I work

  • Protocol before scoring. I write the evaluation rules before any model is scored.
  • Report what did not hold. I retracted a headline metric that a leakage audit showed was circular, and published the correction.
  • Serve only what you verified. The Nyando API checks the model file's SHA-256 before it loads it.
  • Build for the connection you have. I develop mobile-first, on a phone with Termux and Colab, over mobile data.

What I am looking for

Remote machine learning engineering roles, and contract work.

Currently

  • Building Sokoni, a WhatsApp AI sales agent for Kenyan SMEs. The backend is scaffolded and the agent logic is not built yet.
  • Learning retrieval-augmented generation and model fine-tuning. Nothing is shipped in either yet.
