Nyando Flood AI

Flood-susceptibility ranking for five wards of the Nyando Basin, Kenya

In one paragraph

I built a model that ranks locations in five Nyando Basin wards by flood susceptibility, and I tested it against a protocol written before any sealed event was scored. On 27 sealed events it reached a mean per-event AUC of 0.971 (mappable frame) against 0.939 for a model that uses elevation alone. The same protocol shows where the model does not help, and I publish that too.

Problem

Flood-prone areas around the Nyando Basin need a way to rank where water is most likely to reach. The output is a ranking score, not a probability of flooding.

Data

  • 4,420 sampled rows with flood labels derived from Sentinel-1 radar scenes across 35 scene dates.
  • Five wards. Features include terrain, rainfall, soil and land cover.

Method

  • Monotonic-constrained gradient boosting (HistGradientBoosting), so that known physical directions are respected.
  • Exported to ONNX with a custom exporter; served through FastAPI with a React dashboard.
  • Baselines kept in every comparison: an elevation-only model, and a registered logistic model (the champion).

Validation

  • Leave-one-event-out evaluation: mean per-event AUC 0.943 on 23 events.
  • A sealed set of 27 events (D20), scored once, under rules written in advance: a challenger replaces the champion only if the lower end of its interval against the champion is above zero and its mean is higher.
  • Training rows at locations that appear in the sealed events are removed before fitting.

The mappable frame is the protocol's primary analysis. The full frame is shown for completeness.

Against the champion on the full frame, the boosted model gains 0.0164 AUC (interval 0.0052 to 0.0273) and wins on 19 of 27 events.

What the results do not show

  • The champion's gain over elevation alone is not established: the interval on the full frame is -0.0005 to +0.0448, and on the mappable frame it also includes zero.
  • No advantage was shown under a 1 km spatial buffer or an out-of-time split.
  • Scores rank locations. They are not calibrated probabilities.

Engineering

  • Hash-locked Docker installs (31 packages, 483 hashes), CI, CodeQL and MLflow tracking.
  • Release v2.0.0 archived on Zenodo, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23919053.

Correction history

An earlier version reported AUC 0.9717, F1 0.9022 and cross-validation 0.9727 on 2,308 rows. A leakage audit showed those figures were inflated. I retracted them, published an erratum on Zenodo, and rebuilt the project with the protocol above.
